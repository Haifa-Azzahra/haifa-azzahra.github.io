The Broadlane Card Conversation

This is a short course designed to equip you with the skills to confidently pitch the Broadlane Preferred Card, meet daily targets, and build customer trust as Broadlane continues to grow.

Takes about 15–20 minutes. Your choices along the way shape a short debrief at the end.

Why This Matters

Listen for what's behind the pushback, then respond to that concern.
