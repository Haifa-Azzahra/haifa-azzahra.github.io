01 / Problem & Core Insight

The need for speed biting us back in the end.

When managers are stuck in "firefighting" mode, high stress and low emotional regulation lead them to treat AI as an "Easy Button". Aiming for quick speed, they miss critical context and depth. The problem wasn't just AI misuse — it was the state people were in when using AI.

Behavioral Progression & Transformation
Reactive AI Use

TRANSFORMED BY MINDFULNESS →
Mindful AI Use

Read the research insight & academic references +
"Mindfulness acts as a regulatory buffer: it calms AI-driven speed in the emotional-regulation track, and sharpens AI-driven reasoning in the decision-quality track, by enhancing attention, emotional regulation, and awareness of cognitive biases."

Grounded in research by Gibb et al. (2022) on interpretation bias reduction and Lan et al. (2025) on mindfulness shaping structural AI competence and adoption behaviors.

02 / Design Strategy & AI Role

Creating space to think before prompt creation.

AI's Role in Course Design

I leveraged Claude and Perplexity to generate scenario variations and activity sequences. However, suggestions were treated strictly as design hypotheses rather than conclusions.

Design Choice

Emotional regulation was intentionally kept human-led through a dedicated STOP method module prior to AI introduction.

Interactive Decision Chain

Assumption
AI can help process workplace frustration.
Design Decision
Keep emotional regulation human-led.
Implementation
Dedicated chapter to the STOP method.
Outcome
Learners regulate thinking before using AI.

AI accelerates exploration without replacing human design judgment.

02

Emotional regulation must precede cognitive critical evaluation under pressure.

Effective AI learning design focuses on the condition of the user, not just prompt syntax.

A realistic pressure scenario helps learners recognize their own habitual shortcuts.
