My Design Philosophy

"Learning should change what people do — not just what they know."

I design with transfer in mind. Every activity, every assessment, every simulation has a clear behavioural objective — grounded in psychology, tested through data, refined continuously.

01
Diagnose before designing
Needs analysis, learner profiles, and gap mapping come before any slide is built.
02
Science-informed, human-centered
Cognitive load, spaced practice, emotional safety — applied deliberately, not decoratively.
03
Iterate relentlessly
Agile sprints, QA frameworks, and learner feedback loops — every project improves.
